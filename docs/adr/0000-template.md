# NNNN. Title in the imperative, one decision

- Status: proposed | accepted | accepted (retroactive) | superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md) | deprecated
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD (the day the decision was actually taken, from git or the notes; a retroactive ADR keeps that date, not the day it was written)

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, the constraint or the incident that made a decision necessary.
State facts, with their source, not opinions.

## Decision

What was decided, in one or two sentences, then any detail needed to apply it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Alternative A.** What it was, and why it was rejected.
- **Alternative B.** What it was, and why it was rejected.

An alternative with no recorded reason for its rejection says so ("reason not recorded") rather than inventing one.

## Consequences

- Good: what the decision buys.
- Bad: what it costs, what it rules out, what it leaves to watch.

## Sources

- Commits, files (`path:line`), README sections, pull requests or issues the decision is recovered from.
